Why 2ⁿ − 1 moves?

The minimum number of moves grows exponentially with the number of disks. The recurrence below explains why.

2³ − 1 = 7
MINIMUM MOVES
To move n disks from A to C: first move the top n − 1 disks to B; next move the largest disk to C (1 move); finally move the n − 1 disks from B to C.
T(n) = 2T(n − 1) + 1
With T(1) = 1, solving this recurrence gives T(n) = 2ⁿ − 1. Each extra disk nearly doubles the work. That's recursion in action.
